# Chuck Roast

## 1. Asian Chinese-Style Chuck Roast

**Prep Time:** 15 minutes
**Cook Time:** 2.5 hours
**Total Time:** 2 hours 45 minutes

### Ingredients:
- 3 lbs boneless chuck roast
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 3-inch piece fresh ginger, sliced
- 4 scallions, white and green parts separated, chopped
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 cups beef stock
- 1/2 cup Shaoxing wine (or dry sherry)
- 1/3 cup soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons dark soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons oyster sauce
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 3 carrots, cut into 2-inch pieces
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Instructions:
1. Pat the chuck roast dry with paper towels and season with salt and pepper.
2. Heat oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
3. Sear the roast for 5 minutes on each side until well browned. Remove and set aside.
4. In the same pot, add ginger, white parts of scallions, and garlic. Sauté for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
5. Add beef stock, Shaoxing wine, soy sauce, dark soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sugar. Stir to combine.
6. Return the roast to the pot. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low.
7. Cover and simmer for 1 hour 30 minutes.
8. Add carrots and continue cooking for another 1 hour until meat is fork-tender.
9. Remove roast and let rest for 10 minutes before slicing.
10. Garnish with green parts of scallions and serve with the cooking liquid.

## 2. Italian Stracotto (Chuck Roast)

**Prep Time:** 20 minutes
**Cook Time:** 3.5 hours
**Total Time:** 3 hours 50 minutes

### Ingredients:
- 2.5 lbs boneless chuck roast
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 large onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 celery stalks, diced
- 4 oz pancetta, diced
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1 cup red wine (Chianti or Sangiovese)
- 1 cup beef broth
- 1 can (14 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 2 teaspoons fresh rosemary, chopped (or 1/2 teaspoon dried)
- 2 teaspoons fresh thyme, chopped (or 1/2 teaspoon dried)
- 2 bay leaves
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 325°F.
2. Pat the chuck roast dry and season generously with salt and pepper.
3. Heat olive oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
4. Sear the roast on all sides until deeply browned, about 15 minutes total. Remove and set aside.
5. Add pancetta to the pot and cook until crispy, about 5 minutes.
6. Add onion, carrots, and celery. Cook for 7-8 minutes until vegetables are tender.
7. Add garlic and tomato paste, cook for 1 minute.
8. Pour in red wine and bring to a boil, scraping up browned bits from the bottom.
9. Add beef broth, crushed tomatoes, rosemary, thyme, and bay leaves.
10. Return the roast to the pot, cover tightly, and transfer to the oven.
11. Braise for 3-3.5 hours, turning once halfway through, until meat is fork-tender.
12. Remove roast and let rest 10 minutes. Skim fat from sauce if desired.
13. Slice or shred meat and serve with the sauce over polenta or pasta.

## 3. Mediterranean Chuck Roast

**Prep Time:** 10 minutes
**Cook Time:** 4 hours
**Total Time:** 4 hours 10 minutes

### Ingredients:
- 3 lbs chuck roast
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 teaspoons dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1 teaspoon dried rosemary
- 1 teaspoon dried marjoram
- Zest and juice of 1 lemon
- 1/2 cup sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
- 1/2 cup Kalamata olives, pitted
- 1 cup beef broth
- 1/2 cup white wine
- 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 275°F.
2. Season chuck roast with salt and pepper.
3. Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
4. Sear roast on all sides until browned, about 10 minutes. Remove and set aside.
5. In a small bowl, combine remaining olive oil, garlic, oregano, thyme, rosemary, marjoram, lemon zest, and lemon juice.
6. Rub the herb mixture all over the roast.
7. Return roast to the Dutch oven. Add sun-dried tomatoes, olives, beef broth, and white wine.
8. Cover tightly and transfer to the oven.
9. Braise for 4 to 4.5 hours until tender.
10. Remove roast and let rest for 10 minutes.
11. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve with the pan sauce.

## 4. Mexican Barbacoa Chuck Roast

**Prep Time:** 30 minutes
**Cook Time:** 3.5 hours
**Total Time:** 4 hours

### Ingredients:
- 3 lbs chuck roast, cut into 3-inch chunks
- 4 dried guajillo chile peppers, stems and seeds removed
- 3 chipotle peppers in adobo sauce
- 1 large onion, quartered
- 6 cloves garlic
- 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar
- 2 tablespoons lime juice
- 1 tablespoon ground cumin
- 1 tablespoon dried Mexican oregano
- 1 teaspoon ground cloves
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 3 bay leaves
- 1 cup beef broth

### Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 300°F.
2. Place dried guajillo peppers in a bowl and cover with hot water. Let soak for 15 minutes until soft.
3. Drain peppers and add to a blender with chipotle peppers, onion, garlic, vinegar, lime juice, cumin, oregano, cloves, black pepper, and 1/2 cup water.
4. Blend until completely smooth.
5. Season beef chunks with salt and pepper.
6. Heat a large Dutch oven over high heat and sear beef on all sides until browned.
7. Pour the chile sauce over the beef. Add bay leaves and beef broth.
8. Cover tightly and transfer to the oven.
9. Braise for 3-4 hours until beef falls apart when pressed with a fork.
10. Remove beef and shred with two forks.
11. Return shredded meat to the sauce and mix well.
12. Serve in tacos, burritos, or over rice with your favorite toppings.

## 5. French Beef Bourguignon (Chuck Roast)

**Prep Time:** 30 minutes
**Cook Time:** 3 hours
**Total Time:** 3 hours 30 minutes

### Ingredients:
- 3 lbs boneless chuck roast, cut into 2-inch cubes
- 6 oz thick-cut bacon, diced
- 1 large onion, diced
- 2 large carrots, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 8 oz mushrooms, quartered
- 12 pearl onions, peeled
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 1 bottle (750ml) red wine (Pinot Noir or Burgundy)
- 2 cups beef broth
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 4 sprigs fresh thyme
- 2 bay leaves
- 2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 2 tablespoons butter

### Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. In a large Dutch oven, cook bacon over medium heat until crispy, 10-12 minutes. Remove and set aside.
3. Pat beef cubes dry and season with salt and pepper. Toss with flour.
4. Working in batches, brown beef in the bacon fat over medium-high heat, about 8 minutes per batch. Remove and set aside.
5. Add diced onion and carrots to the pot. Cook for 5 minutes.
6. Add garlic and tomato paste. Cook for 1 minute.
7. Pour in red wine and bring to a boil, scraping up browned bits.
8. Add beef broth, thyme, bay leaves, browned beef, and bacon.
9. Cover and transfer to the oven. Braise for 2.5 hours.
10. Meanwhile, in a skillet, sauté mushrooms and pearl onions in butter until golden.
11. Add mushrooms and pearl onions to the pot during the last 30 minutes of cooking.
12. Remove from oven, discard bay leaves and thyme sprigs.
13. Serve over mashed potatoes or with crusty bread.

## 6. Greek Lemon Chuck Roast

**Prep Time:** 15 minutes
**Cook Time:** 3.5 hours
**Total Time:** 3 hours 45 minutes

### Ingredients:
- 3 lbs chuck roast, cut into 6-8 chunks
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- Zest and juice of 2 lemons
- 2 tablespoons dried oregano
- 2 tablespoons fresh rosemary, chopped
- 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- 1 cup water
- 4 russet potatoes, quartered
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 300°F.
2. Season beef chunks generously with salt and pepper.
3. Place beef in a roasting pan.
4. In a bowl, combine olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, lemon juice, oregano, rosemary, and vinegar.
5. Pour the mixture over the beef and toss to coat.
6. Add water to the pan until the meat is half-covered.
7. Cover the pan tightly with foil.
8. Roast for 1.5 hours.
9. Add quartered potatoes, toss to coat with the cooking liquid, and re-cover.
10. Continue cooking for another 2 hours until meat is fork-tender and potatoes are cooked through.
11. Remove foil for the last 15 minutes to brown the top.
12. Serve with the lemon-garlic pan sauce.

## 7. American Classic Pot Roast (Chuck)

**Cook Time:** 3 hours
**Total Time:** 3 hours 25 minutes

- 4 sprigs fresh thyme
- 2 sprigs fresh rosemary

# Rump Roast

## 1. Asian Honey-Soy Rump Roast

**Prep Time:** 5 minutes
**Cook Time:** 6 hours
**Total Time:** 6 hours 5 minutes

### Ingredients:
- 3-4 lbs boneless beef rump roast
- 3 green onions, chopped (white and green parts separated)
- 1/4 cup honey
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 3 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 2 tablespoons fresh ginger, minced
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch mixed with 2 tablespoons water (optional, for thickening)

### Instructions:
1. Place rump roast in a slow cooker.
2. Sprinkle white parts of green onions over the beef.
3. In a bowl, whisk together honey, soy sauce, rice vinegar, ginger, sesame oil, garlic, salt, and pepper.
4. Pour the sauce over the roast, ensuring it's well coated.
5. Cover and cook on low for 6-8 hours until meat is tender.
6. Remove roast and let rest for 10 minutes.
7. If desired, transfer cooking liquid to a saucepan, add cornstarch slurry, and simmer until thickened.
8. Slice roast thinly against the grain.
9. Garnish with green parts of scallions and serve with the sauce.

## 3. Mediterranean Herb Rump Roast

**Prep Time:** 15 minutes
**Cook Time:** 2 hours
**Total Time:** 2 hours 15 minutes

### Ingredients:
- 3 lbs rump roast
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 6 cloves garlic, sliced into slivers
- 3 tablespoons fresh rosemary, chopped
- 2 tablespoons fresh thyme leaves
- 1 tablespoon fresh oregano
- Zest of 1 lemon
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)

### Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 450°F.
2. Remove roast from refrigerator 30 minutes before cooking to bring to room temperature.
3. Make small slits all over the roast and insert garlic slivers.
4. In a bowl, combine olive oil, rosemary, thyme, oregano, lemon zest, lemon juice, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes.
5. Rub the herb mixture all over the roast, massaging it into the meat.
6. Place roast on a rack in a roasting pan.
7. Roast at 450°F for 30 minutes.
8. Reduce temperature to 225°F and continue roasting for 1.5-2 hours until internal temperature reaches 135°F for medium-rare.
9. Remove from oven and tent with foil.
10. Let rest for 15 minutes before slicing.
11. Slice thinly against the grain and serve with pan juices.

6. Smoke brisket fat-side up for 6-8 hours until internal temperature reaches 165°F.
7. Wrap in butcher paper or foil.
8. Continue smoking until internal temperature reaches 200-205°F, another 4-6 hours.
9. Remove and rest for 1 hour.
10. Slice against the grain and serve.

**Oven Method:**
1. Preheat oven to 300°F.
2. Combine all dry spices and rub all over brisket.
3. Heat a large Dutch oven over high heat and sear brisket on both sides.
4. Add beef broth and liquid smoke.
5. Cover tightly and transfer to oven.
6. Braise for 5-6 hours until fork-tender (internal temp 200°F).
7. Remove and let rest 30 minutes.
8. Slice against the grain and serve with pan juices.

## Notes and Tips

### General Cooking Guidelines:
- **Chuck Roast:** Best for slow braising, becomes tender after 3-4 hours
- **Rump Roast:** Can be roasted at high heat or slow-cooked; less marbled than chuck
- **Arm Roast:** Similar to chuck, excellent for pot roast and slow cooking
- **Sirloin Tip Roast:** Lean cut, benefits from marinating; can be roasted quickly
- **Beef Tenderloin:** Premium cut, quick cooking at high heat; very tender
- **Brisket:** Requires long, slow cooking; very flavorful when done properly

### Internal Temperature Guide:
- **Rare:** 120-125°F
- **Medium-Rare:** 130-135°F
- **Medium:** 135-145°F
- **Medium-Well:** 145-155°F
- **Well-Done:** 155°F+
- **Braised/Pot Roast:** 200-205°F (for fall-apart tender)

### Resting Times:
- Tenderloin: 10-15 minutes
- Other roasts: 15-20 minutes
- Large briskets: 30-60 minutes
