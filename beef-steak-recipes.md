## 1. NEW YORK STRIP STEAK

### Asian - Soy-Ginger New York Strip
**Prep Time:** 15 minutes (plus 1 hour marinating)
**Cook Time:** 10 minutes
**Total Time:** 1 hour 25 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 2 New York Strip steaks (10-12 oz each)
- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 1/4 cup orange juice
- 2 tablespoons sherry or rice wine
- 2 tablespoons honey
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons fresh ginger, grated
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 2 green onions, chopped (for garnish)
- 1 tablespoon sesame seeds (for garnish)

**Instructions:**
1. In a bowl, whisk together soy sauce, orange juice, sherry, honey, garlic, ginger, and sesame oil.
2. Place steaks in a large resealable bag and pour marinade over them. Seal and refrigerate for 1 hour (no more than 2 hours).
3. Remove steaks from marinade 30 minutes before cooking to bring to room temperature. Reserve marinade.
4. Pour reserved marinade into a small saucepan and bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer for 5 minutes.
5. Heat grill or cast-iron skillet to high heat (450-500°F).
6. Grill steaks for 4-5 minutes per side for medium-rare (internal temperature 130-135°F).
7. Remove from heat and let rest for 5 minutes.
8. Slice thinly across the grain and drizzle with reduced marinade.
9. Garnish with green onions and sesame seeds.

### Italian - New York Strip with Caprese Salad
**Prep Time:** 15 minutes
**Cook Time:** 15 minutes
**Total Time:** 30 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 2 NY Strip Steaks (10-12 oz each)
- 1/4 cup balsamic vinegar
- 1/2 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons Italian seasoning
- 1/2 teaspoon red chili flakes (optional)
- 1 tablespoon garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon lemon zest
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/2 small red onion, diced
- 1 cup mini fresh mozzarella balls, drained
- 1/2 cup croutons
- 1 tablespoon fresh basil, chopped
- 1 tablespoon fresh oregano, chopped

**Instructions:**
1. Bring steaks to room temperature for 30 minutes and season with salt and pepper.
2. In a bowl, mix balsamic vinegar, olive oil, Italian seasoning, chili flakes, garlic, lemon zest, salt, and pepper.
3. Pour 1/4 cup of marinade over steaks and let sit at room temperature for 5 minutes.
4. In a large salad bowl, mix tomatoes, red onion, mozzarella, croutons, basil, and oregano. Dress with remaining marinade.
5. Preheat cast-iron skillet over high heat until smoking hot.
6. Sear steaks for 2-3 minutes per side until a nice crust forms.
7. Transfer skillet to preheated 400°F oven for 5 more minutes, or until internal temperature reaches 120°F for medium-rare.
8. Remove and let steaks rest for 8 minutes before slicing.
9. Serve alongside the Caprese salad.

### Mediterranean - Herb-Crusted New York Strip
**Prep Time:** 15 minutes
**Cook Time:** 20 minutes
**Total Time:** 35 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 2 New York Strip steaks (10-12 oz each)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 teaspoons fresh rosemary, chopped
- 2 teaspoons fresh thyme, chopped
- 1 tablespoon fresh oregano, chopped
- 1 teaspoon ground coffee
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 2 medium onions, quartered (root intact)
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes
- 2 tablespoons olive oil (for vegetables)

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat grill to medium-high heat (about 400°F).
2. In a small bowl, combine salt, pepper, rosemary, thyme, oregano, and coffee.
3. Rub steaks evenly with the herb mixture.
4. Cut onions into quarters from root to top, keeping core intact. Drizzle onions and tomatoes with olive oil.
5. Place steaks on grill over medium-high heat and cook for 5 minutes per side.
6. Move steaks to indirect heat and continue cooking until internal temperature reaches 125°F for medium-rare (about 5-7 more minutes).
7. Meanwhile, grill onions on two cut sides for 5 minutes each, then finish over indirect heat.
8. Grill tomatoes over high heat for 1-2 minutes until skins are blistered.
9. Remove steaks from grill and let rest for 5 minutes.
10. Serve steaks with grilled onions and tomatoes.

### French - New York Strip au Poivre
**Prep Time:** 10 minutes
**Cook Time:** 15 minutes
**Total Time:** 25 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 2 New York Strip steaks (10-12 oz each, 1-inch thick)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 3 tablespoons unsalted butter, divided
- 4 cloves garlic, smashed
- 4 sprigs fresh thyme
- 2 sprigs fresh rosemary
- 1 teaspoon sea salt
- 2 tablespoons black peppercorns, coarsely crushed
- 1/4 cup cognac or brandy (optional)
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard

**Instructions:**
1. Remove steaks from refrigerator 30 minutes before cooking. Pat dry with paper towels.
2. Season steaks generously with salt, then press crushed peppercorns into both sides.
3. Heat olive oil and 1 tablespoon butter in a large cast-iron skillet over high heat until smoking.
4. Add steaks and cook for 3 minutes without moving.
5. Flip steaks and add garlic, thyme, rosemary, and remaining 2 tablespoons butter.
6. Tilt pan and continuously baste steaks with the herb butter for 2-3 minutes for medium-rare.
7. Remove steaks to a plate and tent with foil. Let rest for 5 minutes.
8. Reduce heat to medium. If using cognac, carefully add to pan and ignite to burn off alcohol (or let simmer for 1 minute).
9. Add heavy cream and Dijon mustard to pan, stirring to combine with pan drippings.
10. Simmer for 2-3 minutes until sauce thickens slightly.
11. Serve steaks with peppercorn cream sauce.

### Greek - Greek Sheet-Pan New York Strip
**Prep Time:** 20 minutes (plus 1 hour marinating)
**Cook Time:** 35 minutes
**Total Time:** 1 hour 55 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 1-1/4 pounds New York Strip steak
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/4 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
- 1 tablespoon plus 2 teaspoons olive oil, divided
- 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 1 tablespoon red wine vinegar
- 2 teaspoons fresh oregano, chopped
- 3/4 teaspoon salt, divided
- 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 1 pound yellow fingerling potatoes, halved lengthwise
- 1 pint grape tomatoes
- 1/2 cup quartered artichoke hearts in water, drained

**Instructions:**
1. In a bowl, whisk together garlic, yogurt, 1 tablespoon oil, lemon juice, honey, vinegar, oregano, 1/4 teaspoon salt, and pepper.
2. Place steak in a large zip-top bag and pour marinade over it. Seal bag and refrigerate for at least 1 hour (up to 8 hours).
3. Preheat oven to 400°F. Spray a rimmed baking pan with cooking spray.
4. Toss potatoes with 1 teaspoon oil and 1/4 teaspoon salt. Spread in single layer on prepared pan.
5. Roast potatoes for 15 minutes.
6. In a bowl, toss tomatoes, artichoke hearts, remaining 1 teaspoon oil, and 1/4 teaspoon salt.
7. Push potatoes to one side of pan. Add vegetable mixture to other side and roast for 5 minutes.
8. Remove steak from marinade and discard marinade. Push vegetables to one side and place steak on other side.
9. Roast for 12 minutes.
10. Turn broiler to high. Broil steak and vegetables for 3 minutes, turning steak once, until internal temperature reaches 145°F.
11. Transfer steak to cutting board, tent with foil, and let stand for 5 minutes.
12. Slice and serve with roasted vegetables.

### American - Classic Pan-Seared New York Strip
**Prep Time:** 5 minutes
**Cook Time:** 10 minutes
**Total Time:** 15 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 2 New York Strip steaks (10-12 oz each, 1-inch thick)
- 2 tablespoons kosher salt
- 1 tablespoon black pepper, coarsely ground
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil or canola oil
- 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 4 cloves garlic, smashed
- 3 sprigs fresh thyme or rosemary

**Instructions:**
1. Remove steaks from refrigerator 30 minutes before cooking to bring to room temperature.
2. Preheat oven to 500°F.
3. Pat steaks completely dry with paper towels.
4. Season both sides generously with kosher salt and black pepper.
5. Heat a well-seasoned cast-iron skillet over high heat until smoking hot (about 3-4 minutes).
6. Add oil to the pan and swirl to coat.
7. Carefully place steaks in the skillet without moving them. Cook for 2 minutes on the first side.
8. Flip steaks and cook for 2 minutes on the second side to create a deep golden crust.
9. Add butter, garlic, and thyme to the pan.
10. Transfer the entire skillet to the preheated oven.
11. Roast for 3-4 minutes for medium-rare (internal temperature 130-135°F) or 4-5 minutes for medium (140-145°F).
12. Remove skillet from oven (use oven mitts!) and transfer steaks to a cutting board.
13. Top each steak with a pat of the garlic herb butter from the pan.
14. Let rest for 5 minutes before serving.

## 2. SKIRT STEAK

### Asian - Teriyaki Skirt Steak Stir-Fry
**Prep Time:** 20 minutes
**Cook Time:** 10 minutes
**Total Time:** 30 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 1-1/2 pounds skirt steak, thinly sliced against the grain
- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 1/4 cup rice vinegar
- 1/4 cup brown sugar
- 2 tablespoons fresh ginger, grated
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons cornstarch mixed with 2 tablespoons water
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil, divided
- 2 cups broccoli florets
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 medium onion, sliced
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- Sesame seeds and green onions for garnish

**Instructions:**
1. In a bowl, whisk together soy sauce, rice vinegar, brown sugar, ginger, and garlic to make the teriyaki sauce.
2. Heat 1 tablespoon vegetable oil in a large wok or skillet over high heat.
3. Add broccoli, bell pepper, and onion. Stir-fry for 3-5 minutes until vegetables are crisp-tender and lightly browned.
4. Remove vegetables from pan and set aside.
5. Add remaining 1 tablespoon oil to the pan.
6. Add sliced skirt steak in a single layer (work in batches if needed).
7. Sear for 2-3 minutes until browned. Don't stir too much to allow proper searing.
8. Return vegetables to the pan with the beef.
9. Pour teriyaki sauce over everything and add the cornstarch slurry.
10. Stir constantly for 1-2 minutes until sauce thickens and coats the meat and vegetables.
11. Drizzle with sesame oil.
12. Garnish with sesame seeds and chopped green onions.
13. Serve over steamed rice or noodles.

### Italian - Skirt Steak with Italian Chimichurri
**Prep Time:** 15 minutes (plus 2 hours marinating)
**Cook Time:** 10 minutes
**Total Time:** 2 hours 25 minutes

**Ingredients:**
**For the steak:**
- 1-1/2 pounds skirt steak
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 3 tablespoons soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons Italian seasoning
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes

**For Italian Chimichurri:**
- 1 cup fresh basil leaves
- 1/2 cup fresh parsley
- 1/4 cup fresh oregano
- 4 cloves garlic
- 1/2 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 3 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes

**Instructions:**
1. Tenderize skirt steak by covering with plastic wrap and pounding with a meat mallet.
2. In a bowl, combine olive oil, soy sauce, lemon juice, Worcestershire, garlic, Italian seasoning, pepper, salt, and red pepper flakes.
3. Place steak in a large zip-lock bag with marinade. Refrigerate for 2 hours or overnight.
4. For chimichurri: Add all ingredients to a food processor and pulse until finely chopped but not pureed. Set aside.
5. Remove steak from refrigerator 30 minutes before cooking.
6. Heat a cast-iron skillet over high heat until sizzling hot.
7. Remove steak from marinade and pat dry.
8. Sear steak for 2-3 minutes per side for medium-rare.
9. Remove from heat and let rest on a cutting board for 10 minutes.
10. Slice thinly against the grain.
11. Serve topped with Italian chimichurri.

### Mediterranean - Mediterranean Grilled Skirt Steak
**Prep Time:** 15 minutes (plus 2 hours marinating)
**Cook Time:** 12 minutes
**Total Time:** 2 hours 27 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 2 pounds skirt steak
- 1/3 cup olive oil
- 2 shallots, chopped
- 4-6 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/3 cup fresh parsley, minced
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon Aleppo pepper or red pepper flakes
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika
- Zest and juice of 1 lemon
- 1/4 cup red wine vinegar
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

**Instructions:**
1. In a bowl, combine shallots, garlic, parsley, cumin, Aleppo pepper, paprika, lemon zest and juice, red wine vinegar, salt, and pepper.
2. Whisk in olive oil until well combined.
3. Place skirt steak in a large non-reactive dish or zip-lock bag.
4. Pour marinade over steak, turning to coat evenly.
5. Marinate for at least 2 hours, up to 24 hours in the refrigerator.
6. Remove steak from refrigerator 30 minutes before grilling.
7. Preheat grill to medium-high heat (about 450°F).
8. Remove steak from marinade and pat dry. Season generously with salt and pepper.
9. Place steak on grill and cook for 4-6 minutes on the first side.
10. Flip and cook for 4-6 minutes on the second side, turning 90 degrees halfway through for crosshatch grill marks.
11. Cook until medium-rare (130-135°F internal temperature).
12. Remove to a cutting board and let rest for 10 minutes.
13. Slice thinly against the grain and serve.

### French - Bistro Skirt Steak with Shallot Butter
**Prep Time:** 15 minutes
**Cook Time:** 15 minutes
**Total Time:** 30 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 1-1/2 pounds skirt steak
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter, divided
- 3 large shallots, thinly sliced
- 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- 1/2 cup beef stock
- 2 sprigs fresh thyme
- 2 tablespoons fresh chives, chopped

**Instructions:**
1. Remove steak from refrigerator at least 30 minutes before cooking. Pat dry with paper towels.
2. Coat steak lightly with olive oil and season liberally with salt and pepper.
3. Heat a cast-iron skillet over high heat until very hot.
4. Add steak and sear for 2 minutes until underside is well browned.
5. Flip steak and cook for 40-50 seconds for medium-rare (skirt steak is thin and cooks very quickly).
6. Remove steak to a cutting board and tent with foil. Let rest for 10 minutes.
7. Reduce heat to medium. Add 2 tablespoons butter to the same skillet.
8. Add sliced shallots and sauté until golden brown, about 7 minutes.
9. Add red wine vinegar and thyme, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan.
10. Add beef stock and simmer until reduced by half, about 3-4 minutes.
11. Remove from heat and stir in remaining 2 tablespoons butter until melted.
12. Slice steak thinly against the grain.
13. Top with shallot butter sauce and garnish with fresh chives.
14. Serve with French fries or haricots verts.

## 3. FLANK STEAK

### Asian - Mongolian Beef with Flank Steak
**Prep Time:** 15 minutes
**Cook Time:** 15 minutes
**Total Time:** 30 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 1-1/2 pounds flank steak, sliced into 1/4-inch strips against the grain
- 1/2 cup cornstarch
- 1/3 cup vegetable oil
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated
- 1/2 cup soy sauce (or tamari for gluten-free)
- 1/2 cup water
- 2/3 cup dark brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
- 4 green onions, cut into 2-inch pieces

**Instructions:**
1. Place sliced flank steak in a large bowl and toss with cornstarch until evenly coated. Shake off excess.
2. Heat vegetable oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat.
3. Working in batches, add beef in a single layer and cook for 30 seconds on each side, just until browned. Transfer to a plate.
4. Remove all but 1 tablespoon oil from the pan.
5. Add minced garlic and ginger to the pan and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
6. Add soy sauce, water, brown sugar, and sesame oil to the pan. Stir well.
7. Bring sauce to a boil and let it boil for 3-5 minutes until slightly thickened.
8. Add the beef back to the pan and toss to coat in the sauce.
9. Add green onions and red pepper flakes if using.
10. Cook for 1-2 more minutes, tossing everything together.
11. Serve immediately over steamed rice.

### Italian - Beef Braciole with Flank Steak
**Prep Time:** 35 minutes
**Cook Time:** 1 hour 30 minutes
**Total Time:** 2 hours 5 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 1-1/2 pounds flank steak
- 4 slices prosciutto
- 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
- 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese, grated
- 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1/4 cup olive oil, divided
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 3 cups marinara sauce
- 1/2 cup red wine
- Butcher's twine
- Salt and pepper to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Place flank steak on a cutting board and use a meat mallet to pound to an even 1/2-inch thickness.
2. Season the flattened steak with salt and pepper.
3. Layer prosciutto slices evenly over the steak.
4. In a bowl, combine breadcrumbs, Parmesan, parsley, garlic, and beaten egg. Mix until it forms a paste.
5. Spread the breadcrumb mixture evenly over the prosciutto.
6. Starting from the short end, tightly roll the steak like a jelly roll.
7. Tie the roll with butcher's twine at 2-inch intervals.
8. Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
9. Sear the braciole on all sides until browned, about 8-10 minutes total. Remove to a plate.
10. Add remaining oil and sauté the onion until softened, about 5 minutes.
11. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute.
12. Pour in red wine and scrape up any browned bits from the bottom.
13. Add marinara sauce and bring to a simmer.
14. Return braciole to the pot, nestling it into the sauce.
15. Cover and reduce heat to low. Simmer for 1-1/2 hours, turning occasionally, until meat is very tender.
16. Remove braciole from sauce and let rest for 10 minutes.
17. Remove twine and slice into 1-inch thick rounds.
18. Serve with the tomato sauce and pasta.

### French - French-Style Flank Steak
**Prep Time:** 15 minutes (plus 12 hours marinating)
**Cook Time:** 20 minutes
**Total Time:** 12 hours 35 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 1-1/2 pounds flank steak
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 shallots, minced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons fresh rosemary, chopped
- 2 tablespoons fresh thyme, chopped
- 1/2 cup dry red wine, divided
- 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 3 tablespoons unsalted butter, cold

**Instructions:**
1. Using a sharp knife, make cross-hatch incisions about 1/4-inch deep on both sides of the flank steak.
2. In a bowl, combine 2 tablespoons olive oil, shallots, garlic, rosemary, thyme, 1/4 cup red wine, Dijon mustard, and pepper.
3. Place steak in a glass dish and pour marinade over it, rubbing it into the cuts.
4. Cover and refrigerate for at least 12 hours, turning once.
5. Remove steak from refrigerator 30 minutes before cooking. Pat dry and season with salt.

- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 3 tablespoons butter, divided
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 large yellow onions, thinly sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 1 (10.5-ounce) can French onion soup
- 1/2 cup beef broth
- 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard

### American - Country Fried Cube Steak with Gravy
**Prep Time:** 15 minutes
**Cook Time:** 20 minutes
**Total Time:** 35 minutes

**Ingredients:**
**For the steak:**
- 6 cube steaks (about 2 pounds)
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional)
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 cup buttermilk
- Vegetable oil for frying (about 1/2 inch deep)

**For the gravy:**
- 3 tablespoons reserved flour from dredging
- 3 tablespoons pan drippings or butter
- 2 cups whole milk
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder

**Instructions:**
1. Set up a breading station: In a shallow dish, combine flour, salt, pepper, garlic powder, paprika, and cayenne. In another dish, whisk together eggs and buttermilk.
2. Dredge each cube steak in flour mixture, then dip in egg mixture, then back in flour mixture for a double coating.
3. Let breaded steaks rest on a wire rack for 5 minutes.
4. Heat about 1/2 inch of vegetable oil in a large cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat to 350°F.
5. Working in batches, carefully add cube steaks to the hot oil.
6. Fry for 3-4 minutes per side until golden brown and crispy.
7. Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate to drain. Keep warm in a 200°F oven while making gravy.
8. For the gravy: Pour off all but 3 tablespoons of oil from the skillet (reserve the browned bits).
9. Add 3 tablespoons of the seasoned flour to the pan drippings.
10. Cook over medium heat, whisking constantly, for 2 minutes until the flour is lightly browned.
11. Gradually whisk in milk, stirring constantly to prevent lumps.
12. Bring to a simmer and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring frequently, until gravy thickens.
13. Season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Taste and adjust seasoning.
14. Serve cube steaks with generous amounts of creamy gravy.
15. Traditional sides include mashed potatoes, green beans, and biscuits.

## Notes

- **Cook Time**: Represents active cooking time on the stove, grill, or in the oven.

  - Rare: 120-125°F
  - Medium-Rare: 130-135°F
