# Specialty Beef Cut Recipes: 7 Cuisines

## 1. Soup Bone (Osso Bucco)

### Italian - Osso Bucco alla Milanese
**Prep Time:** 30 minutes | **Cook Time:** 2.5 hours

**Ingredients:**
- 4 veal shanks (2 inches thick)
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 4 tablespoons olive oil
- 4 tablespoons butter
- 1 large onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 celery stalks, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 1/2 cups dry white wine
- 1 cup beef stock
- 1 can (14 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 2 bay leaves
- 4 sprigs fresh thyme
- Salt and pepper to taste

**Gremolata:**
- 1/2 cup fresh parsley, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- Zest of 1 lemon

**Instructions:**
1. Season shanks with salt and pepper, dredge in flour, shaking off excess
2. Heat oil and butter in Dutch oven over medium-high heat, brown shanks on all sides (3-4 minutes per side), remove and set aside
3. In same pot, sauté onion, carrots, and celery for 5 minutes until softened
4. Add garlic, cook 1 minute
5. Pour in wine, scraping up browned bits, simmer 3 minutes
6. Add stock, tomatoes, bay leaves, and thyme
7. Return shanks to pot, bring to boil
8. Cover and transfer to 325°F oven for 2.5 hours until meat is tender
9. Mix gremolata ingredients
10. Serve shanks with sauce, topped with gremolata

### French - Osso Bucco Provençal
**Prep Time:** 35 minutes | **Cook Time:** 3 hours

**Ingredients:**
- 4 veal shanks
- 1/2 cup flour
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 3 carrots, sliced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 cups dry white wine
- 2 cups beef stock
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 2 strips orange zest
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 3 sprigs fresh rosemary
- 2 sprigs fresh thyme
- 2 bay leaves
- 1/2 cup pitted black olives
- Salt and pepper

**Instructions:**
1. Season and flour shanks
2. Heat oil in Dutch oven, brown shanks 4 minutes per side, remove
3. Sauté onion and carrots 5 minutes
4. Add garlic, cook 1 minute
5. Stir in tomato paste, cook 2 minutes
6. Add wine, simmer 5 minutes
7. Add stock, tomatoes, orange zest, and herbs
8. Return shanks to pot, bring to simmer
9. Cover, braise at 325°F for 2.5 hours
10. Add olives, cook uncovered 30 minutes
11. Serve with crusty bread

### Asian - Asian Braised Osso Bucco
**Prep Time:** 25 minutes | **Cook Time:** 3 hours

**Ingredients:**
- 4 veal shanks
- 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 onion, sliced
- 6 cloves garlic, smashed
- 2-inch piece ginger, sliced
- 1/2 cup Shaoxing wine
- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons dark soy sauce
- 3 tablespoons brown sugar
- 4 star anise
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 2 cups beef stock
- 2 tablespoons hoisin sauce
- 1 teaspoon five-spice powder
- 4 scallions, cut into 2-inch pieces
- Steamed bok choy for serving

**Instructions:**
1. Heat oil in large pot, brown shanks on all sides, remove
2. Sauté onion, garlic, and ginger for 3 minutes
3. Add Shaoxing wine, scraping up browned bits
4. Add both soy sauces, brown sugar, star anise, cinnamon, stock, hoisin, and five-spice
5. Return shanks to pot, bring to boil
6. Reduce heat, cover, simmer 2.5-3 hours until tender
7. Add scallions in last 5 minutes
8. Serve with steamed rice and bok choy

## 2. Beef Liver

### Asian - Asian Stir-Fried Beef Liver
**Prep Time:** 35 minutes (includes soaking) | **Cook Time:** 8 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 1 lb beef liver, sliced thin
- 1 cup milk (for soaking)
- 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1-inch ginger, julienned
- 1 bell pepper, sliced
- 1 onion, sliced
- 2 scallions, cut into 2-inch pieces

**Marinade:**
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon Shaoxing wine
- 1 teaspoon cornstarch
- 1/2 teaspoon sesame oil

**Sauce:**
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon oyster sauce
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 1/4 cup water

**Instructions:**
1. Soak liver in milk for 30 minutes, drain and pat dry
2. Mix marinade ingredients, marinate liver 10 minutes
3. Mix sauce ingredients
4. Heat wok over high heat with oil
5. Stir-fry liver 2 minutes until just cooked, remove
6. Add garlic and ginger, stir-fry 30 seconds
7. Add pepper and onion, stir-fry 2 minutes
8. Return liver, add sauce, stir-fry 1 minute
9. Add scallions, toss, serve immediately with rice

### Italian - Fegato alla Veneziana
**Prep Time:** 40 minutes (includes soaking) | **Cook Time:** 15 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 1 lb calf's liver (or beef liver), sliced thin
- 1 cup milk
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 3 large onions, thinly sliced
- 2 tablespoons white wine vinegar
- 2 tablespoons white wine
- 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped
- Salt and pepper
- Polenta for serving

**Instructions:**
1. Soak liver in milk for 30 minutes, drain, pat dry
2. Heat 2 tablespoons each butter and oil in large skillet
3. Cook onions over medium-low heat 10-12 minutes until very soft and golden
4. Remove onions, keep warm
5. Increase heat to medium-high
6. Add remaining butter and oil
7. Season liver, cook 1-2 minutes per side until browned but still pink inside
8. Return onions to pan
9. Add vinegar and wine, cook 1 minute
10. Garnish with parsley, serve immediately with polenta

### French - Foie de Boeuf à la Moutarde
**Prep Time:** 35 minutes | **Cook Time:** 10 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 1 lb beef liver, sliced
- 1 cup milk
- 1/2 cup flour
- 4 tablespoons butter
- 2 shallots, minced
- 1/2 cup white wine
- 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard

- 2 tablespoons fresh parsley

**Instructions:**

6. Add wine, simmer 2 minutes

### American - Liver and Onions with Bacon
**Prep Time:** 35 minutes | **Cook Time:** 20 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 1 lb beef liver, sliced
- 1 cup milk
- 6 strips bacon
- 2 large onions, sliced
- 1/2 cup flour
- 1 cup beef broth
- 2 tablespoons butter
- Salt and pepper

**Instructions:**
1. Soak liver in milk 30 minutes, drain
2. Cook bacon until crisp, remove, crumble
3. Cook onions in bacon fat 10 minutes, remove
4. Season liver, coat with flour
5. Brown liver in bacon fat 3 minutes per side, remove
6. Add butter and any remaining flour to pan
7. Whisk in broth, simmer until thickened
8. Return liver and onions, warm through
9. Top with bacon, serve with mashed potatoes

## 3. Beef Tongue

### Asian - Chinese Braised Beef Tongue
**Prep Time:** 20 minutes | **Cook Time:** 3 hours

**Ingredients:**
- 1 beef tongue (3-4 lbs)
- 6 cups water
- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 1/4 cup dark soy sauce
- 1/4 cup Shaoxing wine
- 3 tablespoons brown sugar
- 6 slices ginger
- 6 cloves garlic, smashed
- 4 star anise
- 2 cinnamon sticks
- 1 tablespoon Sichuan peppercorns
- 4 scallions, cut into pieces
- 2 bay leaves

**Instructions:**
1. Rinse tongue, place in large pot with water
2. Bring to boil, reduce heat, simmer 2.5 hours
3. Remove tongue, peel off skin while hot
4. In clean pot, combine soy sauces, wine, sugar, and 4 cups water
5. Add ginger, garlic, star anise, cinnamon, peppercorns, scallions, and bay leaves
6. Add peeled tongue, bring to boil
7. Reduce heat, simmer 30 minutes
8. Let cool in braising liquid
9. Slice and serve cold with sauce

### French - Langue de Boeuf Sauce Gribiche
**Prep Time:** 40 minutes | **Cook Time:** 3 hours

**Ingredients:**
- 1 beef tongue (3-4 lbs)
- 1 onion, halved
- 2 carrots
- 2 bay leaves
- Salt and peppercorns

**Sauce Gribiche:**
- 3 hard-boiled eggs
- 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard
- 1/4 cup white wine vinegar
- 3/4 cup olive oil

### Greek - Tongue with Lemon Sauce
**Prep Time:** 25 minutes | **Cook Time:** 3 hours

**Ingredients:**
- 1 beef tongue (3-4 lbs)
- 1 onion, quartered
- 2 carrots
- 2 bay leaves
- Salt and peppercorns

**Lemon Sauce:**
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 3 tablespoons flour
- 2 cups reserved cooking broth
- 2 egg yolks
- Juice of 2 lemons
- Salt and pepper

**Instructions:**
1. Boil tongue with vegetables and seasonings for 3 hours
2. Reserve 2 cups broth
3. Peel tongue, slice

7. Temper eggs with hot sauce, then combine
8. Pour over sliced tongue, serve with rice

### American - Pickled Beef Tongue
**Prep Time:** 30 minutes (plus 5 days brining) | **Cook Time:** 3 hours

**Brine:**
- 2 quarts water
- 1 cup salt
- 1/2 cup brown sugar
- 2 tablespoons pickling spice
- 6 cloves garlic
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 teaspoon pink curing salt

**Ingredients:**
- 1 beef tongue (3-4 lbs)
- 1 onion, quartered
- 2 carrots
- 2 celery stalks

**Instructions:**
1. Combine brine ingredients, bring to boil, cool completely
2. Place tongue in brine, refrigerate 5-7 days
3. Remove from brine, rinse
4. Place in pot with vegetables, cover with water
5. Simmer 3 hours until tender
6. Peel while hot
7. Slice and serve warm or cold on sandwiches with mustard
