## Bone-In Ham Steak

### Asian: Sticky Asian Glazed Ham Steak

**Prep Time:** 10 minutes
**Cook Time:** 15 minutes
**Total Time:** 25 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 2 bone-in ham steaks (8 oz each)
- 2 tablespoons sesame oil
- 2 tablespoons minced garlic
- 1 tablespoon minced fresh ginger
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 1/4 cup honey
- 3 tablespoons brown sugar
- 2 tablespoons oyster sauce
- 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
- 1 tablespoon butter
- 2 green onions, sliced
- 1 teaspoon sesame seeds

**Instructions:**
1. In a small saucepan over medium heat, add sesame oil, minced garlic, and minced ginger. Cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
2. Add soy sauce, honey, brown sugar, oyster sauce, and apple cider vinegar. Stir until combined.
3. Continue cooking for 8-10 minutes until the sauce has reduced by about half and thickens to a syrup consistency.
4. In a large skillet, melt butter over medium heat. Add ham steaks and cook for 3-4 minutes per side until lightly browned.
5. Brush the glaze generously over both sides of the ham steaks.
6. Cook for an additional 2-3 minutes per side, continuing to baste with glaze until sticky and caramelized.
7. Garnish with sliced green onions and sesame seeds before serving.

### Italian: Ham Steak with Marsala Sauce

**Prep Time:** 10 minutes
**Cook Time:** 20 minutes
**Total Time:** 30 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 2 bone-in ham steaks (8 oz each)
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 leeks, white parts only, sliced
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 1 cup Marsala wine
- 1/2 cup water
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper
- Fresh parsley, chopped

**Instructions:**
1. Heat butter and olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
2. Add ham steaks and brown for 2 minutes per side. Remove and set aside.
3. In the same skillet, add sliced leeks and sauté until softened, about 5 minutes.
4. Sprinkle flour over the leeks and stir for 1 minute.
5. Slowly add Marsala wine and water, stirring constantly to prevent lumps.
6. Bring to a simmer and cook until sauce thickens, about 5 minutes.
7. Return ham steaks to the pan and cook for 3-4 minutes until heated through.
8. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
9. Garnish with fresh parsley before serving.

### Mediterranean: Lemon Herb Ham Steak

**Prep Time:** 10 minutes
**Cook Time:** 15 minutes
**Total Time:** 25 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 2 bone-in ham steaks (8 oz each)
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- Zest of 1 lemon
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon fresh oregano, chopped (or 1 teaspoon dried)
- 1 tablespoon fresh thyme leaves
- 1 tablespoon fresh rosemary, chopped
- 1 teaspoon honey
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/4 cup kalamata olives, pitted and halved
- 2 tablespoons capers

**Instructions:**
1. In a small bowl, whisk together olive oil, lemon zest, lemon juice, minced garlic, oregano, thyme, rosemary, honey, and black pepper.

3. Add ham steaks and cook for 3-4 minutes per side until browned.
4. Add the remaining herb mixture, olives, and capers to the pan.
5. Cook for 2-3 minutes, spooning the sauce over the ham steaks.
6. Serve immediately with the pan sauce drizzled over the top.

### Mexican: Chipotle Pineapple Ham Steak

**Prep Time:** 10 minutes
**Cook Time:** 20 minutes
**Total Time:** 30 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 2 bone-in ham steaks (8 oz each)
- 1 can (8 oz) pineapple rings, juice reserved
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- 1 chipotle pepper in adobo, minced
- 1 tablespoon adobo sauce
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon chili powder
- Juice of 1 lime
- Fresh cilantro, chopped
- Salt to taste

**Instructions:**
1. In a bowl, whisk together reserved pineapple juice, brown sugar, minced chipotle, adobo sauce, cumin, chili powder, and lime juice.
2. Melt butter in a large skillet over medium heat.
3. Add ham steaks and cook for 2-3 minutes per side until browned. Remove and set aside.
4. In the same skillet, add pineapple rings and cook for 1 minute per side.
5. Pour the chipotle-pineapple sauce into the skillet and bring to a simmer.
6. Return ham steaks to the pan and cook for 3-4 minutes, basting frequently with the sauce.
7. Cook until the sauce thickens and becomes sticky.
8. Serve ham topped with grilled pineapple and garnish with fresh cilantro.

### French: Ham Steak with Dijon Cream Sauce

**Prep Time:** 10 minutes
**Cook Time:** 15 minutes
**Total Time:** 25 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 2 bone-in ham steaks (8 oz each)
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 shallots, finely minced
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 3 tablespoons Dijon mustard
- 1 tablespoon whole grain mustard
- 1 tablespoon fresh tarragon, chopped
- Salt and white pepper to taste
- Fresh chives, chopped

**Instructions:**
1. Melt butter in a large skillet over medium heat.
2. Add ham steaks and cook for 3 minutes per side until golden. Remove and keep warm.
3. In the same skillet, add minced shallots and sauté for 2 minutes until softened.
4. Pour in white wine and simmer for 3 minutes to reduce by half.
5. Stir in heavy cream, Dijon mustard, and whole grain mustard.
6. Simmer for 4-5 minutes until sauce thickens slightly.
7. Add fresh tarragon and season with salt and white pepper.
8. Return ham steaks to the pan and coat with sauce.
9. Garnish with fresh chives before serving.

## Pork Ribs

### Asian: 1-2-3-4-5 Chinese Ribs

**Prep Time:** 15 minutes
**Cook Time:** 30 minutes
**Total Time:** 45 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 2 lbs pork ribs, cut into 1.5-2 inch pieces
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 3 slices fresh ginger
- 1 tablespoon Shaoxing wine
- 2 tablespoons light soy sauce
- 3 tablespoons Chinese black vinegar
- 4 tablespoons sugar
- 5 tablespoons water
- 2 green onions, sliced
- Sesame seeds for garnish

**Instructions:**
1. Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a large skillet or wok over medium heat.
2. Add ginger slices and fry for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add pork ribs, turn heat to high, and brown on all sides for 4-5 minutes.
4. In a bowl, mix together Shaoxing wine, soy sauce, black vinegar, sugar, and water (the 1-2-3-4-5 sauce).
5. Pour sauce over the ribs and bring to a boil.
6. Reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes until ribs are tender.
7. Remove lid and increase heat to reduce sauce until only 1/4 cup remains and ribs are glazed.
8. Garnish with sliced green onions and sesame seeds.

### Italian: Braised Pork Ribs with Tomatoes and Olives

**Prep Time:** 20 minutes
**Cook Time:** 2.5 hours
**Total Time:** 2 hours 50 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 3 lbs pork ribs, cut into individual ribs
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons fresh rosemary, chopped
- 6 fresh sage leaves
- 1 cup red wine
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 cup black olives, pitted
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 325°F.
2. Season ribs generously with salt and pepper.
3. Heat olive oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
4. Brown ribs on all sides, working in batches, about 8-10 minutes total. Remove and set aside.
5. Add onions to the pot and cook until lightly browned, about 8 minutes.
6. Add garlic, rosemary, and sage; cook for 2 minutes.
7. Pour in red wine and bring to a rapid boil for 3-4 minutes.
8. Stir in crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, and olives.
9. Return ribs to the pot, bring to a boil, then cover and transfer to the oven.
10. Braise for 2-2.5 hours until meat is tender and falling off the bone.
11. Remove from oven and let rest for 10 minutes before serving.

### Mediterranean: Lemon Oregano Pork Ribs

**Prep Time:** 20 minutes (plus marinating time)
**Cook Time:** 2 hours
**Total Time:** 2 hours 20 minutes (plus marinating)

**Ingredients:**
- 3 lbs pork ribs
- 1/2 cup olive oil
- Juice of 2 lemons
- Zest of 1 lemon
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 tablespoons fresh oregano, chopped (or 2 tablespoons dried)
- 2 tablespoons fresh thyme
- 2 tablespoons honey
- 1 tablespoon white vinegar
- 1 tablespoon sea salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 2 bay leaves
- 8 juniper berries (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. In a large bowl, whisk together olive oil, lemon juice, lemon zest, garlic, oregano, thyme, honey, vinegar, salt, pepper, and cinnamon.
2. Place ribs in a large dish and pour marinade over, turning to coat. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes or up to 24 hours.
3. Preheat oven to 325°F.
4. Remove ribs from marinade and place in a roasting pan. Add bay leaves and juniper berries.
5. Pour marinade over ribs, cover tightly with aluminum foil.
6. Roast for 1.5 hours, then remove foil and increase temperature to 375°F.
7. Roast uncovered for 30 minutes more, basting occasionally, until ribs are tender and glazed.
8. Let rest for 10 minutes before serving.

### Mexican: Pork Ribs in Salsa Roja

**Prep Time:** 20 minutes
**Cook Time:** 50 minutes
**Total Time:** 1 hour 10 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 2.5 lbs pork ribs, cut into individual pieces
- 6 guajillo chiles, stems and seeds removed
- 3 medium tomatoes, halved
- 1/2 head garlic, cloves separated
- 1/4 cup vinegar
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 2-3 teaspoons salt
- 1 cup water
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil

**Instructions:**
1. Toast the dried guajillo chiles in a dry skillet for 1-2 minutes until fragrant. Set aside.
2. Add chiles, tomatoes, 1 cup water, vinegar, and salt to a blender. Blend until smooth, about 1 minute.
3. Place ribs in a large pot with just enough water to almost cover. Add the garlic cloves.
4. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer. Cook for 20-30 minutes until water evaporates.
5. Add vegetable oil to the pot and brown the ribs, about 5 minutes.
6. Pour the salsa roja over the ribs and add sugar.
7. Bring to a simmer and cook uncovered for 20 minutes, stirring every 5 minutes, until sauce has reduced by half.
8. Serve with rice and beans.

### French: Pork Ribs with Herbs de Provence

**Prep Time:** 15 minutes
**Cook Time:** 2.5 hours
**Total Time:** 2 hours 45 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 3 lbs pork ribs
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 tablespoons Herbes de Provence
- 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup dry white wine
- 1 cup chicken stock
- 2 tablespoons balsamic vinegar
- 2 tablespoons honey
- 1 tablespoon fresh mint, chopped
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh thyme sprigs

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 300°F.
2. In a small bowl, combine olive oil, Herbes de Provence, Dijon mustard, minced garlic, salt, and pepper.
3. Rub mixture all over the ribs.
4. Heat a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Sear ribs for 5 minutes on each side until browned.
5. Remove ribs and add white wine to deglaze the pan, scraping up browned bits.
6. Add chicken stock, balsamic vinegar, and honey. Bring to a simmer.
7. Return ribs to the pot, add thyme sprigs, and cover.
8. Braise in the oven for 2-2.5 hours until tender.
9. Remove ribs and reduce sauce on the stovetop for 10 minutes.
10. Garnish with fresh mint before serving.

### Greek: Greek Lemon Pork Ribs

**Prep Time:** 15 minutes (plus marinating time)
**Cook Time:** 1.5 hours
**Total Time:** 1 hour 45 minutes (plus marinating)

**Ingredients:**
- 3 lbs baby back ribs
- 4 tablespoons fresh oregano, chopped (or 2 tablespoons dried)
- 1/2 cup sweet onion, finely chopped
- 6 garlic cloves, minced
- Zest from 1 lemon
- 1/3 cup fresh lemon juice
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 2 tablespoons honey
- 1 tablespoon sea salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme

**Instructions:**
1. In a large bowl, whisk together oregano, onion, garlic, lemon zest, lemon juice, olive oil, honey, salt, pepper, and thyme.
2. Cut ribs into individual pieces, trimming off excess fat.
3. Place ribs in a large resealable bag, pour marinade over, and seal. Refrigerate for at least 8 hours or overnight, turning occasionally.
4. Preheat oven to 400°F.
5. Arrange ribs on a rack in a foil-lined roasting pan. Pour marinade over ribs.
6. Roast for 10 minutes, then reduce temperature to 375°F.
7. Continue roasting for 1 hour, turning once and basting occasionally.
8. Increase temperature to 400°F and roast for final 15 minutes until meat is tender and almost falling off the bone.

### American: Classic BBQ Oven-Baked Ribs

**Prep Time:** 15 minutes
**Cook Time:** 3 hours
**Total Time:** 3 hours 15 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 3 lbs baby back ribs

**Dry Rub:**
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon smoked paprika
- 1 tablespoon garlic powder
- 1 tablespoon onion powder
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon dry mustard
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper

**BBQ Sauce:**
- 1 cup ketchup
- 1/4 cup brown sugar
- 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
- 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tablespoon hot sauce (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 275°F.
2. Remove the membrane from the back of the ribs by sliding a knife under it and peeling away.
3. Combine all dry rub ingredients in a bowl.
4. Rub the spice mixture generously over both sides of the ribs.
5. Wrap ribs tightly in aluminum foil and place on a baking sheet.
6. Bake for 2.5 hours until tender.
7. Meanwhile, combine all BBQ sauce ingredients in a saucepan and simmer for 10 minutes.
8. Remove ribs from oven, discard foil, and brush both sides generously with BBQ sauce.
9. Increase oven temperature to broil. Broil ribs for 3-4 minutes until sauce begins to caramelize.
10. Let rest for 5 minutes before cutting and serving.

## Pork Sausage

### Asian: Korean-Style Sausage Stir Fry

**Prep Time:** 10 minutes
**Cook Time:** 10 minutes
**Total Time:** 20 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 1 lb pork sausage, sliced into rounds
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 green bell pepper, sliced
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon fresh ginger, grated

**Sauce:**
- 2 tablespoons gochujang (Korean red chili paste)
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 1 teaspoon rice vinegar
- 2 green onions, sliced
- 1 tablespoon sesame seeds

**Instructions:**
1. In a small bowl, whisk together gochujang, soy sauce, honey, sesame oil, and rice vinegar. Set aside.
2. Heat vegetable oil in a large wok or skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Add sausage rounds and cook for 3 minutes until browned. Remove and set aside.
4. In the same pan, add bell peppers and onions. Stir-fry for 3-4 minutes until slightly softened.
5. Add garlic and ginger; cook for 1 minute.
6. Return sausage to the pan and reduce heat to medium.
7. Pour sauce over the mixture and toss to coat.
8. Cook for 2 minutes until sauce thickens.
9. Garnish with sliced green onions and sesame seeds.
10. Serve over rice.

### Italian: Sausage and Peppers Pasta

**Prep Time:** 10 minutes
**Cook Time:** 25 minutes
**Total Time:** 35 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 1 lb Italian pork sausage, casings removed
- 12 oz pasta (penne or rigatoni)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 yellow bell pepper, sliced
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- Fresh basil leaves
- Grated Parmesan cheese

**Instructions:**
1. Cook pasta according to package directions. Reserve 1 cup pasta water before draining.
2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Add sausage, breaking it up with a wooden spoon. Cook for 5-7 minutes until browned. Remove and set aside.
4. In the same pan, add peppers and onions. Sauté for 5 minutes until softened.
5. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute.
6. Pour in white wine and simmer for 2 minutes.
7. Add crushed tomatoes, oregano, red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper.
8. Return sausage to the pan and simmer for 10 minutes.
9. Add cooked pasta and toss to combine, adding pasta water as needed for desired consistency.
10. Garnish with fresh basil and Parmesan cheese.

### Mexican: Homemade Chorizo

**Prep Time:** 20 minutes
**Cook Time:** 10 minutes
**Total Time:** 30 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 2 lbs ground pork (70/30 fat ratio)
- 3 tablespoons chili powder
- 2 tablespoons smoked paprika
- 1 tablespoon ground cumin
- 1 tablespoon dried oregano
- 2 teaspoons ground coriander
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cloves
- 1 tablespoon salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar
- 2 tablespoons water

**Instructions:**

### French: Quick Sausage Cassoulet

**Prep Time:** 10 minutes
**Cook Time:** 50 minutes
**Total Time:** 1 hour

**Ingredients:**
- 1 lb pork sausage (garlic sausage or kielbasa), sliced
- 4 slices bacon, chopped
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 carrots, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 cans (15 oz each) cannellini beans, drained
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 2 bay leaves
- 2 teaspoons fresh thyme
- 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
- 2 tablespoons butter, melted
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh parsley, chopped

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. In a large Dutch oven, cook bacon over medium heat until crispy. Remove and set aside.
3. Add sausage slices to the pot and brown for 5 minutes. Remove and set aside.

7. Return bacon and sausage to the pot.
8. Bring to a simmer, season with salt and pepper.
9. Mix breadcrumbs with melted butter and sprinkle over the top.
10. Transfer to oven and bake uncovered for 30 minutes until breadcrumbs are golden.
11. Garnish with fresh parsley before serving.

### American: Sausage Gravy

**Prep Time:** 5 minutes
**Cook Time:** 15 minutes
**Total Time:** 20 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 1 lb pork breakfast sausage
- 1/3 cup all-purpose flour
- 3 cups whole milk
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
- Pinch of cayenne pepper (optional)
- Biscuits for serving

**Instructions:**
1. Place sausage in a large skillet over medium-high heat.

## Bratwurst

### Asian: Teriyaki Bratwurst Stir Fry

**Prep Time:** 15 minutes
**Cook Time:** 15 minutes
**Total Time:** 30 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 4 bratwurst sausages, sliced into 1-inch pieces
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 yellow bell pepper, sliced
- 1 cup snap peas
- 1 cup broccoli florets
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon fresh ginger, grated

**Teriyaki Sauce:**
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons honey
- 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 1 teaspoon cornstarch
- 2 tablespoons water
- 1 tablespoon sesame seeds
- 2 green onions, sliced

**Instructions:**
1. In a small bowl, whisk together soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar, sesame oil, cornstarch, and water. Set aside.
2. Heat 1 tablespoon vegetable oil in a large wok or skillet over high heat.
3. Add bratwurst pieces and cook for 5-6 minutes until browned. Remove and set aside.
4. Add remaining oil to the pan. Add bell peppers, snap peas, and broccoli.
5. Stir-fry for 4-5 minutes until vegetables are crisp-tender.
6. Add garlic and ginger; cook for 1 minute.
7. Return bratwurst to the pan.
8. Pour teriyaki sauce over everything and toss to coat.
9. Cook for 2 minutes until sauce thickens.
10. Garnish with sesame seeds and sliced green onions.
11. Serve over rice or noodles.

### Mediterranean: Herb-Marinated Grilled Bratwurst

**Prep Time:** 40 minutes (including marinating)
**Cook Time:** 15 minutes
**Total Time:** 55 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 4 bratwurst sausages

### French: Pork Tenderloin with Dijon Cream Sauce

**Prep Time:** 10 minutes
**Cook Time:** 30 minutes
**Total Time:** 40 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 1.5 lbs pork tenderloin
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper

**Dijon Cream Sauce:**
- 2 shallots, minced
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 3 tablespoons Dijon mustard
- 1 tablespoon whole grain mustard
- 1 tablespoon fresh tarragon, chopped
- Salt and white pepper to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Season tenderloin generously with salt and pepper.
3. Heat butter and olive oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat.
4. Sear tenderloin on all sides until golden, about 8 minutes.
5. Transfer to oven and roast for 15-20 minutes until internal temperature reaches 145°F.
6. Remove pork and let rest (keep warm).
7. Return skillet to stovetop over medium heat.
8. Add shallots and sauté for 2 minutes.
9. Pour in white wine and simmer for 3 minutes to reduce.
10. Stir in heavy cream and both mustards.
11. Simmer for 5 minutes until sauce thickens.
12. Add tarragon and season with salt and white pepper.
13. Slice pork and serve with sauce.

### American: Classic Roasted Pork Tenderloin

**Prep Time:** 5 minutes
**Cook Time:** 30 minutes
**Total Time:** 35 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 1.5 lbs pork tenderloin
- 2 tablespoons olive oil

**Seasoning:**
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon smoked paprika
- 1 tablespoon garlic powder
- 1 tablespoon onion powder
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Remove silver skin from tenderloin and pat dry with paper towels.
3. In a small bowl, mix all seasoning ingredients.
4. Brush tenderloin with olive oil.
5. Rub seasoning mixture all over the tenderloin, coating completely.
6. Heat an oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat.
7. Sear tenderloin on all sides until golden brown, about 6-8 minutes total.
8. Transfer skillet to oven.
9. Roast for 15-20 minutes until internal temperature reaches 145°F.
10. Remove from oven and tent with foil.
11. Let rest for 10 minutes before slicing.
12. Slice into 1/2-inch medallions and serve.
