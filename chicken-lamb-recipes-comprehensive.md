## WHOLE CHICKEN

### 1. Asian: Chinese Five-Spice Roasted Chicken

**Prep Time:** 20 minutes (plus marinating)
**Cook Time:** 70 minutes
**Total Time:** 90 minutes (plus marinating)

**Ingredients:**
- 1 whole small chicken (3-4 lbs)
- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 3 tablespoons oyster sauce
- 6 tablespoons Shaoxing wine
- 1 pinch of salt
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1 tablespoon five-spice powder
- 2 stalks scallions
- 5 slices ginger
- 6 cloves garlic

**Instructions:**
1. In a bowl, combine soy sauce, oyster sauce, Shaoxing wine, salt, sugar, five-spice powder, scallions, ginger, and garlic. Mix well.
2. Place chicken in a large resealable bag and pour marinade over it. Seal bag and massage marinade evenly into chicken.
3. Marinate in refrigerator for 24-48 hours, flipping occasionally.
4. Preheat oven to 400°F (204°C).
5. Remove chicken from marinade and place on a rack in roasting pan.
6. Roast at 400°F for 30 minutes.
7. Rotate pan and roast for another 20 minutes.
8. Reduce oven temperature to 250°F (121°C) and roast for final 20 minutes.
9. Check internal temperature reaches 165°F in thickest part of thigh.
10. Let rest 10 minutes before carving.

### 2. Italian: Roasted Chicken with Lemon, Garlic & Rosemary

**Prep Time:** 15 minutes
**Cook Time:** 90 minutes
**Total Time:** 105 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 1 whole chicken (3-4 lbs), bone-in, skin-on
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 2 lemons (1 for zest/juice, 1 cut into quarters)
- 6 whole garlic cloves
- 2 tablespoons fresh rosemary, chopped
- 2 teaspoons Italian seasoning
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon paprika

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
2. Pat chicken dry with paper towels.
3. Mix olive oil, lemon zest, lemon juice (from 1 lemon), Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper in small bowl.
4. Place chicken on aluminum foil-lined pan.
5. Rub olive oil mixture all over chicken, including under the skin.
6. Stuff cavity with lemon quarters, garlic cloves, and rosemary sprigs.
7. Sprinkle paprika over outside of chicken.
8. Roast at 425°F for 30 minutes.
9. Reduce temperature to 375°F and continue roasting for 30-45 minutes.
10. Check internal temperature reaches 165°F in thickest part of thigh.
11. Let rest 10-15 minutes before carving.

### 3. Mediterranean: Herb and Lemon Roasted Chicken

**Prep Time:** 15 minutes (plus marinating)
**Cook Time:** 75 minutes
**Total Time:** 90 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 1 whole chicken (3-4 lbs)
- 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 3 tablespoons lemon juice
- 2 teaspoons lemon zest
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons dried oregano

### 4. Mexican: Pollo Asado (Mexican Roast Chicken)

**Prep Time:** 20 minutes (plus marinating)
**Cook Time:** 60 minutes
**Total Time:** 80 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 1 whole chicken (3-4 lbs), spatchcocked
- 1/2 cup orange juice
- 1/4 cup lime juice
- 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 1/4 cup fresh cilantro
- 1 tablespoon achiote powder
- 1 tablespoon white wine vinegar
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 teaspoons dried oregano (Mexican oregano preferred)
- 2 teaspoons ground cumin
- 1 tablespoon paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes

**Instructions:**
1. To spatchcock: Place chicken breast-side down, cut along both sides of backbone and remove it.
2. Flip chicken over and press down firmly on breastbone to flatten.
3. Combine all marinade ingredients in blender and blend until smooth.
4. Place chicken in large resealable bag, pour marinade over, seal and massage into meat.
5. Marinate for at least 1 hour (up to overnight in refrigerator).
6. Preheat oven to 400°F (or preheat grill to 350-400°F).
7. Remove chicken from marinade and place on baking sheet or grill.
8. Roast/grill for 60-70 minutes until internal temperature reaches 165°F.
9. Baste with remaining marinade every 15 minutes.
10. Let rest 10 minutes before cutting into pieces.

### 5. French: Poulet Rôti (Classic French Roast Chicken)

**Prep Time:** 25 minutes
**Cook Time:** 90 minutes
**Total Time:** 115 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 1 whole chicken (3-4 lbs)
- 1/4 cup (57g) butter, at room temperature
- 2 tablespoons Herbes de Provence (or mix of thyme, rosemary, marjoram)
- 2 lemons (1 for zest, both cut in half)
- 6 sprigs fresh thyme
- 2 cloves garlic, unpeeled but smashed
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 teaspoons flaky sea salt
- 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 2-3 cups root vegetables (carrots, parsnips, potatoes), roughly chopped
- 2 medium yellow onions, thickly sliced

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
2. Mix softened butter with Herbes de Provence and lemon zest.
3. Pat chicken completely dry with paper towels.
4. Carefully loosen skin from breast and thighs, slide herb butter under skin.
5. Stuff cavity with lemon halves, smashed garlic, and thyme sprigs.
6. Rub outside of chicken with olive oil, season generously with salt and pepper.
7. Arrange vegetables and onions in bottom of roasting pan.
8. Place chicken on top of vegetables.
9. Roast for 1 hour 30 minutes, basting every 20 minutes with butter and pan drippings.
10. Check internal temperature reaches 165°F in thickest part of thigh.
11. Let rest 10-15 minutes before carving.

## CHICKEN BONE-IN THIGHS

### 1. Asian: Teriyaki Chicken Thighs

**Prep Time:** 10 minutes
**Cook Time:** 30 minutes
**Total Time:** 40 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 6-8 bone-in chicken thighs, skin-on
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons mirin
- 2 tablespoons sake
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- 2 tablespoons honey
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 2 tablespoons water
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- Sesame seeds for garnish
- Sliced green onions for garnish

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
2. In bowl, whisk together soy sauce, mirin, sake, sugar, brown sugar, honey, garlic, and ginger.
3. Pat chicken thighs dry and place in baking dish.
4. Pour half of teriyaki sauce over chicken.
5. Bake for 25 minutes.
6. Meanwhile, heat remaining sauce in small saucepan.
7. Mix cornstarch with water, add to sauce and simmer until thickened.
8. After 25 minutes, flip chicken and brush with thickened sauce.
9. Bake additional 25 minutes, basting every 8-10 minutes.
10. Check internal temperature reaches 165°F.
11. Garnish with sesame seeds and green onions before serving.

### 2. Italian: Chicken Cacciatore (Braised Chicken Thighs)

**Prep Time:** 20 minutes
**Cook Time:** 45 minutes
**Total Time:** 65 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 6 bone-in chicken thighs, skin-on
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 yellow bell pepper, sliced
- 8 oz mushrooms, sliced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1/2 cup dry red wine
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 2 teaspoons dried oregano
- 2 teaspoons dried basil
- 1 teaspoon dried rosemary
- 2 bay leaves
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh basil for garnish

**Instructions:**
1. Season chicken thighs with salt and pepper.
2. Heat olive oil in large Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
3. Brown chicken thighs skin-side down for 5 minutes, flip and brown 3 more minutes. Remove and set aside.
4. In same pot, add onion, peppers, and mushrooms. Cook 5 minutes until softened.
5. Add garlic and cook 1 minute until fragrant.
6. Add tomato paste, stir and cook 1 minute.
7. Pour in wine to deglaze pan, scraping up browned bits.
8. Add crushed tomatoes, chicken broth, oregano, basil, rosemary, and bay leaves.
9. Return chicken to pot, nestling into sauce.
10. Bring to simmer, cover and cook 30-40 minutes until chicken reaches 165°F.
11. Remove bay leaves, garnish with fresh basil and serve.

### 5. French: Coq au Vin

**Prep Time:** 25 minutes
**Cook Time:** 60 minutes
**Total Time:** 85 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 8 bone-in chicken thighs and drumsticks
- 4 oz thick-cut bacon, chopped
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 cups red wine (Burgundy or Pinot Noir)
- 1 cup chicken stock
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 10 oz pearl onions, peeled
- 8 oz cremini mushrooms, halved
- 3 sprigs fresh thyme
- 2 bay leaves
- 2 tablespoons butter
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh parsley, chopped

**Instructions:**
1. Season chicken pieces with salt and pepper, dredge lightly in flour.
2. In large Dutch oven, cook bacon over medium heat until crispy. Remove and set aside.
3. In bacon fat, brown chicken pieces in batches, about 5 minutes per side. Remove and set aside.
4. Add garlic to pot, cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
5. Pour in wine to deglaze, scraping up browned bits.
6. Add chicken stock, tomato paste, thyme, and bay leaves. Stir to combine.
7. Return chicken and bacon to pot.
8. Bring to simmer, cover and cook 30 minutes.
9. Meanwhile, in separate pan, sauté pearl onions and mushrooms in butter until golden, about 8 minutes.
10. Add vegetables to pot, continue simmering uncovered for 20-30 minutes until chicken reaches 165°F.
11. Remove bay leaves and thyme sprigs.
12. Garnish with fresh parsley before serving.

## CHICKEN BONELESS BREAST

### 1. Asian: Ginger Soy Chicken Stir-Fry

**Prep Time:** 20 minutes
**Cook Time:** 10 minutes
**Total Time:** 30 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 1.5 lbs boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 3 tablespoons soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 2 tablespoons honey
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 1/4 cup chicken broth
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 2 cups broccoli florets
- 1 carrot, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated
- 2 green onions, sliced
- Sesame seeds for garnish

**Instructions:**
1. In bowl, whisk together soy sauce, rice vinegar, honey, sesame oil, cornstarch, and chicken broth. Set aside.
2. Heat 1 tablespoon vegetable oil in large skillet or wok over high heat.
3. Add chicken pieces, season with salt and pepper. Cook 4-5 minutes until lightly browned. Remove and set aside.
4. Add remaining tablespoon oil to pan.
5. Add bell pepper, broccoli, and carrot. Stir-fry 3-4 minutes until tender-crisp.
6. Add garlic and ginger, cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
7. Return chicken to pan.
8. Pour sauce over everything, toss to coat.
9. Cook 2-3 minutes until sauce thickens and chicken is cooked through (165°F).
10. Garnish with green onions and sesame seeds.
11. Serve over rice.

### 2. Italian: Chicken Piccata

**Prep Time:** 10 minutes
**Cook Time:** 15 minutes
**Total Time:** 25 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 3 tablespoons butter, divided
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 1/2 cup chicken broth
- 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice
- 1 tablespoon lemon zest
- 1/4 cup capers, drained
- 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped

**Instructions:**
1. Cut each chicken breast in half horizontally to create 8 thin cutlets.
2. Pound cutlets to even 1/4-inch thickness.
3. Mix flour, salt, pepper, and garlic powder in shallow dish.
4. Dredge each cutlet in flour mixture, shaking off excess.
5. Heat 1 tablespoon butter and olive oil in large skillet over medium-high heat.
6. Cook cutlets in batches, 3-4 minutes per side until golden. Remove and keep warm.
7. Add wine to pan, scraping up browned bits. Cook until reduced by half, about 2 minutes.
8. Add chicken broth, lemon juice, and lemon zest. Bring to simmer.
9. Stir in capers and remaining 2 tablespoons butter.
10. Return chicken to pan, simmer 2-3 minutes to heat through.
11. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve immediately.

### 5. French: Chicken Dijon (Poulet Dijonnaise)

**Prep Time:** 10 minutes
**Cook Time:** 18 minutes
**Total Time:** 28 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 3 tablespoons butter, divided
- 1/3 cup shallots, finely diced
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 1/2 cup chicken broth
- 3 tablespoons Dijon mustard
- 1 tablespoon whole grain mustard
- 1/2 cup heavy cream (or crème fraîche)
- 1 tablespoon fresh tarragon, chopped
- 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves

**Instructions:**
1. Pound chicken breasts to even thickness (about 3/4 inch).
2. Season flour with salt and pepper, dredge chicken in seasoned flour.
3. Melt 2 tablespoons butter in large skillet over medium-high heat.
4. Cook chicken 4-5 minutes per side until golden and cooked through (165°F). Remove and keep warm.
5. Add remaining tablespoon butter to pan, add shallots and cook 2 minutes until softened.
6. Pour in wine, scraping up browned bits. Cook until reduced by half, about 2 minutes.
7. Add chicken broth, bring to simmer.
8. Whisk in Dijon mustard, whole grain mustard, and heavy cream.
9. Simmer 3-4 minutes until sauce thickens slightly.
10. Stir in tarragon and thyme.
11. Return chicken to pan, spoon sauce over and heat through.
12. Serve immediately with sauce spooned over chicken.

### 6. Greek: Moussaka

**Prep Time:** 45 minutes
**Cook Time:** 60 minutes
**Total Time:** 105 minutes

**Ingredients:**

**For the eggplant:**
- 2 large eggplants, sliced into 1/3-inch rounds
- Salt for sweating
- Olive oil for brushing

**For the meat sauce:**
- 2 lbs ground lamb
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 large onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1/2 cup red wine
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon white pepper
- 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped

**For the béchamel sauce:**
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 4 tablespoons flour
- 2 cups whole milk, warmed
- 1/2 teaspoon nutmeg
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/8 teaspoon white pepper
- 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese
- 1 egg, lightly beaten

**Instructions:**
1. Salt eggplant slices, let sit 30 minutes. Rinse and pat dry.
2. Brush eggplant with olive oil and bake at 400°F for 25 minutes until golden. Set aside.
3. Heat olive oil in large skillet, cook onion 5 minutes.
4. Add garlic, cook 1 minute.
5. Add ground lamb, cook until browned, about 8 minutes.
6. Add tomatoes, tomato paste, wine, sugar, salt, cinnamon, peppers, and parsley.
7. Simmer 20 minutes until thickened. Set aside.
8. For béchamel: Melt butter in saucepan, whisk in flour. Cook 1 minute.
9. Gradually whisk in warm milk. Cook, stirring constantly, until thickened.
10. Remove from heat, stir in nutmeg, salt, pepper, and Parmesan.
11. Let cool slightly, then whisk in beaten egg.
12. Preheat oven to 350°F.
13. Layer half the eggplant in baking dish.
14. Spread meat sauce over eggplant.
15. Layer remaining eggplant on top.
16. Pour béchamel sauce evenly over top.
17. Bake 60 minutes until golden and bubbling.
18. Let rest 15 minutes before slicing and serving.
